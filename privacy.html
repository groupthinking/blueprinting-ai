Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains how Blueprinting (“we,” “us,” “our”) handles information in connection with our website, blueprinting.ai, our free pilot request form, and our Missed Call Text Back service, which we provide on behalf of local trade businesses (each, “the Business”).

Information we collect

Pilot request form

When a shop owner or manager requests a free pilot on blueprinting.ai, we collect the shop name, your name, your mobile number, your trade, and whether you checked the optional text-message consent box, along with the date and time of the request. We use this information only to contact you about your pilot request: to schedule your intro call, set up and test the service, and answer your questions. We do not use it for any other purpose, and we do not sell it.

Missed Call Text Back

We collect the phone number of callers to participating Businesses, the content of text conversations, and basic call and message metadata (such as timestamps). We use this information only to deliver the text-back service and to let the Business reply to your inquiry.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you check the optional text-message box on our pilot request form, Blueprinting may text you at the mobile number you gave about your pilot (scheduling, setup and test updates, and support). Callers to a participating Business may receive texts as described in our SMS Terms. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

How we share information

  • We do not sell or share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.
  • We share information only with the Business you contacted and with service providers we need to deliver messages (such as our SMS carrier), and only to operate the service or when required by law.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to opt out of text messages at any time.
  • Reply HELP for help.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Message frequency varies.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed to provide the service or meet legal requirements.
