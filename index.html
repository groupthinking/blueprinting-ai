How it works

Set up in about a day. Runs on its own after that.

No new phone and nothing to install. It works alongside the number your customers already call.

  1. 1

    A call goes unanswered

    Someone calls your shop’s usual number and nobody can get to the phone in time. The call forwards to your dedicated Blueprinting text line.

  2. 2

    The caller gets a text

    Within seconds, that line texts them back with your shop’s name, letting them know you got their call and asking what they need.

  3. 3

    You get the conversation

    Their reply goes to your phone with the details of the job, so you can respond when you’re free.

  4. 4

    The lead is logged

    Every missed call and text thread is saved, so you can see who reached out and follow up on anyone you haven’t booked.

Texting and privacy

Responsible business texting, built in.

Texts go only to people who called your shop first, and every customer can opt out at any time. We follow carrier rules for business texting.

Caller-initiated only

A text is sent only in reply to a call the customer placed to your shop. Consent is not a condition of purchase, and we don’t send marketing messages through this service.

STOP and HELP handled automatically

Every first text includes Reply STOP to opt out. Replying STOP ends messages after one confirmation; HELP returns contact details. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply.

Your customers’ data stays private

We do not sell or share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Information is used only to run the service for your shop.

FAQ

Questions shop owners ask.

Don’t see yours? Email hello@blueprinting.ai.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Customers keep calling the number they already have. Calls you don’t answer forward to a dedicated local Blueprinting text line, which texts the caller back with your shop’s name in the message. The text comes from that line, not from your shop’s own number.

What does the text say?

Your shop’s name and a short question about what the caller needs. We set the wording with you during onboarding, and every first text includes Reply STOP to opt out.

Will it work with my cell phone?

Yes. Most carriers support conditional call forwarding, which sends only the calls you don’t pick up to your dedicated text line. We set it up with you.

We use a landline, VoIP, or software like ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro or Jobber. Does this work?

Most phone systems support no-answer or busy forwarding, or a ring-group overflow, to another number. We set that up with you during onboarding and test it before the pilot starts. Your dispatch software keeps working as it does today. If your system can’t forward, we’ll tell you on the intro call.

Will it text back robocalls or telemarketers?

We only text calls that ring through and go unanswered. We set rules with you during onboarding, such as skipping toll-free numbers and sending at most one text to a repeat caller in a set window. Anyone can reply STOP.

How fast is setup?

Usually within a day.

What does it cost?

The first 14 days are free. After that it’s $150 per month per shop, with no contract. Cancel anytime.

Is texting customers allowed?

Yes, when the customer called you first and every text offers a way to opt out. Texts only go to people who called your shop, STOP and HELP are handled automatically, and we follow carrier rules for business texting.

What happens to my customers’ information?

We use caller numbers and messages only to run the service for your shop. We never sell or share mobile numbers or opt-in data for marketing. See our Privacy Policy.
