09The basics

Missed-call text-back, explained.

Missed-call text-back is simple: when a call to your shop goes unanswered, the caller automatically gets a text from your business within seconds. Instead of hitting voicemail and moving on, they can reply with what’s wrong and where they are, and you get the details on your phone.

For plumbers, HVAC techs and electricians in Kansas City, that matters because most calls are urgent and most callers won’t wait. A burst pipe in Overland Park or a dead furnace in Lee’s Summit goes to whichever shop responds first.

When you’re on a ladder or under a house, a fast, friendly text keeps that customer talking to you, not the next listing on Google. It works with your existing number through call forwarding, so nothing changes for your customers except they hear back faster.

SMS Terms and Consent

Blueprinting provides Missed Call Text Back on behalf of local trade businesses ("the Business"). When you call a participating Business and the call is missed, you may receive a text message from that Business's phone number in reply to your call.

Consent

By calling a participating Business and replying to its text message, you agree to receive conversational text messages from that Business about your inquiry, such as service details, scheduling, and follow-up. Consent is not a condition of purchase. We do not send marketing messages through this service.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies based on your conversation with the Business. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-out and help

Reply STOP to opt out Reply HELP for help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. You will get one confirmation text and no more messages after that. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at hayden@blueprinting.ai or (628) 304-9759.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Blueprinting handles information in connection with Missed Call Text Back.

Information we collect

We collect the phone number of callers to participating Businesses, the content of text conversations, and basic call and message metadata (such as timestamps). We use this information only to deliver the text-back service and to let the Business reply to your inquiry.

How we share information

  • We do not sell or share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.
  • Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.
  • We share information only with the Business you contacted and with service providers we need to deliver messages (such as our SMS carrier), and only to operate the service or when required by law.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to opt out of text messages at any time.
  • Reply HELP for help.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Message frequency varies.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed to provide the service or meet legal requirements.
