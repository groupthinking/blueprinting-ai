Terms of Service

Last updated: November 9, 2026

These terms describe the Missed Call Text Back service provided by Blueprinting (“we,” “us,” “our”) to local trade businesses (each, “the Business”), and the text messages callers may receive through it.

The service

When a call to a participating Business goes unanswered, the call may forward to a dedicated Blueprinting text line, and the caller may receive a text message from that line identifying the Business by name. The Business’s own phone number does not change. Replies are sent to the Business so it can respond, and missed calls and conversations are logged for the Business.

Pilot, billing and cancellation

Businesses may start with a free 14-day pilot. After the pilot, the service is $150 per month per shop. There is no long-term contract, and a Business may cancel at any time by contacting support@blueprinting.ai or calling (913) 444-6493.

Acceptable use

The service is used only to reply to people who called the Business first. Businesses agree not to use it to send marketing messages or to text anyone who has opted out.

SMS Terms and Consent

Blueprinting runs two text-messaging programs: (1) Blueprinting Pilot Texts, sent by Blueprinting to shop owners and managers who request a free pilot, and (2) Missed Call Text Back, sent on behalf of a participating Business to people who called it.

Blueprinting Pilot Texts (to shop owners)

Program name: Blueprinting Pilot Texts. How you opt in: by checking the optional, unchecked-by-default text-message box on the pilot request form at blueprinting.ai/#start-pilot. You can request a pilot without checking it. What we send: texts about your pilot: scheduling your intro call, onboarding and setup steps, call-forwarding and test-call results, go-live and pilot-end notices, and replies to your support questions, plus follow-ups about missed calls logged for your shop during the pilot. We do not send marketing messages. Frequency: message frequency varies, typically a few texts per week during setup and the pilot. Cost: message and data rates may apply. Opt out: reply STOP at any time; you will get one confirmation text and no more messages. Help: reply HELP, or email hello@blueprinting.ai or call (913) 444-6493. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Missed Call Text Back (to callers)

Blueprinting provides Missed Call Text Back on behalf of local trade businesses (“the Business”). When you call a participating Business and the call is missed, you may receive a text message in reply to your call from a dedicated Blueprinting text line used on behalf of that Business, identifying the Business by name.

Consent

By calling a participating Business and replying to its text message, you agree to receive conversational text messages from that Business about your inquiry, such as service details, scheduling, and follow-up. Consent is not a condition of purchase. We do not send marketing messages through this service.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies based on your conversation with the Business. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-out and help

Reply STOP to opt outReply HELP for help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. You will get one confirmation text and no more messages after that. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at support@blueprinting.ai or (913) 444-6493.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in data are never sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties. See our Privacy Policy.
